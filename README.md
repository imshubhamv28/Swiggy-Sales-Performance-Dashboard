## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing food delivery data to uncover key business insights and build an interactive Power BI dashboard.  
The dashboard helps in understanding sales performance, customer behavior, and top-performing restaurants and dishes.

---

## 📊 Key Metrics
- Total Sales: ₹53M  
- Total Orders: 197K+  
- Average Order Value: ₹268  
- Average Rating: 4.34 ⭐  

---

## 🔍 Dashboard Features
- Analysis of top-performing restaurants, cities, and states  
- Weekly and monthly sales trends  
- Top 10 dishes contributing to revenue  
- Customer rating distribution  
- Interactive filters (Day, City, State, Month)

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- Bengaluru is the highest revenue-generating city  
- Sales peak during weekends, especially Saturday  
- Most ratings fall between 4.3 – 4.5, indicating consistent service quality  
- A few restaurants and dishes contribute majorly to overall revenue
